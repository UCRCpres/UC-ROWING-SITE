UC Rowing Club

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The University of Canterbury Rowing Club (UCRC) is committed to protecting the privacy of our members. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Who we are

UC Rowing Club is a student-run sports club affiliated with the University of Canterbury, based in Christchurch, New Zealand. You can contact us at our contact page.

What information we collect

When you create a member account or join the club, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Year of study
  • Student ID (if provided)

When you make a payment through our website, the payment is processed by Blink Pay (a New Zealand payment service). We do not see or store your bank account details — Blink Pay handles this directly. We do store a record of what was paid and when.

If you appear in photos uploaded to our gallery, your image may be stored and displayed publicly on this website.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Manage your club membership
  • Track and collect club fees
  • Send you information about club events and activities
  • Comply with University of Canterbury club requirements

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share your information with

Your information is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. We share information with the following third parties only as necessary to operate the club:

  • Blink Pay — to process fee payments (they receive your name and the payment amount)
  • Supabase — our database host (they store your member data on our behalf)

We do not share your information with any other third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

How we protect your information

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption)
  • Your member data is stored in a secure database with access controls — members can only see their own information
  • Only club administrators can access the full member list
  • API keys and secrets are stored securely and never included in publicly accessible code

How long we keep your information

We keep your member record for the duration of your membership and for a reasonable period afterwards in case of disputes or queries. If you would like your data deleted, please contact us and we will remove it within a reasonable timeframe.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct any inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information (subject to any legal obligations)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we've handled your personal information, please contact us first and we'll do our best to resolve it. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
